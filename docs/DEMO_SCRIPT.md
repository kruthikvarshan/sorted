## Recording the voice-over
- iPhone **Voice Memos**, quiet room, phone ~20 cm from your mouth, no music or fan.
- One memo per scene, named `VO1` … `VO10`. Pause 1 second before and after each line. Redo any line freely.
- AirDrop the 10 memos to the Mac (Downloads). I time the cut to your voice.
