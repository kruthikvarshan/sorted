# Sorted: 3-minute voice-over (for the AI narrator)

Tone: warm, confident, a little playful; a friend telling you about something they love. "…" = a short breath pause.
Target ≈ 430 words ≈ 3:00 with the visuals. One file per scene (VO1–VO11).

**VO1 · The problem (0:00–0:16)**
Seven thousand six hundred messages. Rent. Trips. Bets. Birthdays. … Every group chat has one. And every group chat has the same problem. Ask one simple question… *who owes whom?* … and watch everyone go quiet.

**VO2 · Meet Sorted (0:16–0:42)**
Meet Sorted. A new member of your group chat, powered by Meta AI. … Start a group, your friends scan a QR code, and you just talk… in your own language, the way you always do. Hindi, Telugu, English… or all three in one sentence. Sorted reads along, and quietly turns the chatter into what's decided… chai at five-thirty… and who owes what. One-fifty each, to me.

**VO3 · Every rupee has a receipt (0:42–1:12)**
This is our real flat group. Four months. Every topic… sorted. Every rupee… settled in the fewest possible payments. Tap any amount, and you land on the exact message it came from. No guesses. No made-up numbers. … It even catches the arguments: Aniket wants a cottage, Swapnil wants a hostel. And anyone can just ask. *How much does Aniket owe me?* … Eleven thousand five hundred. Manali, plus the AC. Meta AI reads the chat. Our code does the maths.

**VO4 · Paid? Prove it. (1:12–1:28)**
Aniket pays on UPI and drops the screenshot. Sorted reads it, checks it against the bill, and I confirm in two words. … And if someone sends the same screenshot twice? … Caught.

**VO5 · Nudges that land (1:28–1:42)**
Need a gentle nudge? Sorted sends a real WhatsApp reminder. The phone buzzes… one tap… and you're right back in the group, as yourself.

**VO6 · Bring any chat (1:42–1:52)**
Already drowning in an old group? Just screenshot it. Sorted reads the conversation straight from the image.

**VO7 · Plans that plan themselves (1:52–2:14)**
Planning a trip? Sorted already knows what the group decided. Where. When. How much. … And it shows you the message behind each one. It asks only for what's missing… then searches the web for stays, routes, Instagram spots… all within your budget, with real sources.

**VO8 · Talk, don't type (2:14–2:30)**
Don't feel like typing? Send a voice note. Or put the phone down and just… plan out loud. Sorted knows who said what. … And when it's all done? Group Wrapped. Painted by Meta AI.

**VO9 · My Sorted (2:30–2:40)**
And My Sorted is just for you. Across every group: what you owe, what you're owed… and an answer to anything you ask. Privately.

**VO10 · What's inside (2:40–2:58) · slide: feature → Meta model**
So what's inside? … Muse Spark reads every message and every screenshot, in any language. Muse Voice hears your voice notes, and tells the voices apart. Muse Image paints your Wrapped. Meta AI's web search plans the trip. And the WhatsApp Cloud API delivers the reminders. … Meta AI does the reading. Our code does the maths: the model proposes, the code verifies. Hundreds of tests, and every call traced.

**VO11 · Why Meta, why now (2:58–3:12) · slide: "Ready to ship inside every WhatsApp group"**
Billions of people already run their lives in WhatsApp groups. Everything Sorted needs, Meta already has. … Meta could ship this inside every group… tomorrow. … Sorted. Group chats that actually get things done.
